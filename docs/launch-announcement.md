# SignFlow Beta — Launch Announcement Templates

Copy/paste-ready messages to announce the beta to your team. Pick the channel and customise the bracketed bits.

---

## 1. Email announcement (HTML-safe plain text)

**Subject:** Introducing HQHB · SignFlow — your account is ready

```
Hi team,

Today we're rolling out HQHB · SignFlow — our new internal tool for
document approvals. Instead of WhatsApping PDFs around or emailing
scanned signatures, you'll submit, track, and download signed copies
all in one place.

Request. Review. Approve. Track. All in one place.


WHAT YOU CAN DO

  - Submit any document for approval (PDF or Excel, up to 14 MB)
  - Route to a specific team OR a chain of signers
  - Get a clean, signed PDF back with full audit trail
  - Approve documents from anywhere — desktop, tablet, or phone


GETTING STARTED

  1. You'll receive a separate welcome email with your sign-in
     credentials (look out for "Welcome to HQHB SignFlow").

  2. Sign in here: https://onesign.devhqhb.online

  3. On first sign-in, you'll be asked to register your signature —
     either draw it (best on touch devices) or upload an image.

  4. Change your password from the top nav once you're in — pick
     something you'll remember.


GUIDES

  We've put together a short user guide covering everything from
  submitting your first request to approving on mobile. Attached
  to this email. ~5 minute read.

  Find the QUICK REF card pinned to your team's space — one-pager
  with everything you need day-to-day.


SUPPORT

  Questions, bugs, anything broken — ping me directly or email
  it@hqhb.in.

  This is a beta. Treat it like one — the more feedback you send,
  the faster it improves.

Welcome aboard.

— Taha
   IT Admin, HQHB
```

## 5. Approver-specific note

For users you've added as approvers — they need to know they're going to start receiving requests.

**Subject:** You're set up as an Approver on SignFlow — what that means

```
Hi [Name],

I've added you as an Approver on the [Team Name] team in HQHB · SignFlow.

This means: when anyone submits a document that needs sign-off from
[Team Name], you'll get an email notification and the request will
appear in your "Pending approvals" tab.

WHAT TO DO RIGHT NOW

  1. Sign in: https://onesign.devhqhb.online (credentials in the
     welcome email).
  2. Register your signature — required before you can approve
     anything. Draw it on a touchpad or upload a scanned image.

WHEN A REQUEST COMES IN

  Open the doc → review → click "Preview & approve" to see your
  signature on the page → click "Confirm approval".

  Or "Reject" with a reason — the requestor gets your feedback.

  Once you approve, there's a 1-hour cooling window where you can
  withdraw the approval if you change your mind.

User guide: [attach / link]
Questions: it@hqhb.in.

— Taha
```
