## A. All staff

**Subject:** Your SignFlow Handbook — now with self-registration & direct signing

Hi everyone,

Attached is the complete **SignFlow Handbook** — everything you need to use HQHB's digital signature and approval system in one place: a quick-start, role-by-role guides (Requestor, Approver, Administrator), onboarding steps, and an FAQ.

**Two new things worth knowing** (see **What's New** on page 2):

- **Create your own account.** New joiners can request access right from the sign-in page — click **Create an account**, enter your details, and IT approves it. You're in once approved.
- **Send a signature request to anyone.** When you make a request, choose **Send to a specific person**, search a colleague by **name or email**, and request their signature directly — no matter which team they're on. It shows up for them under **Awaiting your signature**.

Keep the handbook handy as your reference. Sign in any time at **onesign.devhqhb.online**.

Questions? Contact IT at **it@hqhb.in** (or **taha.chunawala@hqhb.in**).

Thanks,
**IT — HQHB**
