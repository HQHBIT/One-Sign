# Announcement email — SignFlow launch

**Subject:** Introducing SignFlow — faster approvals, fully digital signatures

---

Hi everyone,

We're rolling out **SignFlow**, HQHB's new digital signature and approval system — one place to send documents for signature, sign and approve them online, and keep a complete, auditable record of every approval.

**Why we're introducing it**
Getting a document signed today means printing, scanning, chasing people over email, and hoping nothing gets lost. SignFlow replaces all of that:

- Submit any PDF or Excel document for signature in seconds
- Route it to the right approver(s) — a single sign-off or a multi-step chain, in order
- Approvers review and sign digitally; every step is time-stamped and audit-ready
- Nothing slips through the cracks — you always know what a document is waiting on

**What it means for you**
- **Most of you** will use SignFlow to *submit* documents for approval and download the signed copies.
- **Approvers / authorised signatories** will *review and sign* the documents routed to you.
- It runs in your web browser — there's nothing to install.

**Getting started**
1. IT will set up your account — you'll receive your **login email and a temporary password**.
2. Open **https://onesign.devhqhb.online/** and sign in.
3. Change your password, then **register your signature** once (draw it or upload an image).
4. That's it — you're ready to go.

**Guides & help**
Step-by-step guides are attached — a one-page Quick-Start, role-by-role walkthroughs, and an FAQ. Short demo videos are on the way. If you need a hand, contact **IT at it@hqhb.in** (or **taha.chunawala@hqhb.in**).

Thank you,
**IT — HQHB**
